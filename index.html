Blood tells you about the whole body. Bronchoalveolar lavage tells you about the airway itself, but a patient can only tolerate it a few times a year. The cell in between — airway-local and repeatable — is currently empty.

呼氣冷凝液（Exhaled Breath Condensate, EBC）是填這一格的候選方法：受試者對著冷凝管靜息呼吸約十分鐘，呼出氣體中的水蒸氣與來自氣道內襯液的微小液滴一起凝結成幾百微升的液體。非侵入、不需鎮靜、可以在門診椅上完成。

Exhaled breath condensate (EBC) is a candidate for that cell. The subject breathes quietly into a cooled tube for about ten minutes; water vapour and micro-droplets carried up from the airway lining fluid condense together into a few hundred microlitres of liquid. Non-invasive, no sedation, done in an outpatient chair.

它補的是「頻率」這一格——而這一格目前沒有別的東西。 It will not replace blood draws, and it will not replace lavage.
What it adds is frequency — and nothing else currently fills that gap.

01 ・ 基本定義01 ・ Definition

什麼是呼氣冷凝液？What is exhaled breath condensate?

歐洲呼吸學會（ERS）2017 年技術標準的定義是：「EBC 是讓呼出氣體接觸冷表面或冷凝器降溫後所取得的樣本，以液態或凍結狀態收集，用於分析其中的揮發性與非揮發性大分子。」

The European Respiratory Society 2017 technical standard defines it as: “EBC is obtained by cooling exhaled breath through contact with a cold surface or condenser. Samples are collected as fluid or frozen material and analysed immediately or later for volatile and non-volatile macromolecules.”

關鍵在於「它從哪裡來」。呼出氣流通過氣道時，剪切力會把覆蓋在氣道上皮表面的內襯液（airway lining fluid）撕出微小液滴，這些液滴隨氣流帶出體外。冷凝液裡絕大部分體積是水蒸氣，但真正有臨床意義的訊號，來自這些微滴所攜帶的蛋白、細胞因子與核酸。

The key question is where it comes from. As exhaled air travels through the airways, shear forces tear micro-droplets from the airway lining fluid coating the epithelium, and those droplets are carried out with the airstream. Most of the condensate volume is water vapour — but the clinically meaningful signal comes from what those droplets carry: proteins, cytokines and nucleic acids.

它不是唾液It is not saliva

唾液是 EBC 最主要的污染源。採樣時以唾液阻隔設計加上「定期吞嚥」控制——這是 ERS 技術標準明文的操作要求，不是選配。

Saliva is the main contaminant. It is controlled by a saliva trap plus periodic swallowing during collection — an explicit requirement in the ERS standard, not an optional extra.

它不是痰It is not sputum

不需要高滲鹽水誘導、不需要咳出。病人只要靜息呼吸，因此重度阻塞或無法配合誘導痰的病人也能採。

No hypertonic saline induction, no expectoration. The patient only breathes quietly — so it is feasible in patients who cannot tolerate sputum induction.

它不是「一口氣」It is not “one breath”

這是最常見的誤解。EBC 是連續數分鐘的靜息潮氣呼吸；單次呼氣的水量遠不足以分析。把它說成「吹一口氣」會直接誤導臨床端對流程的預期。

The most common misunderstanding. EBC requires several minutes of continuous tidal breathing; a single exhalation yields far too little fluid. Calling it a “single breath test” misleads clinicians about the workflow.

它目前不是診斷工具It is not a diagnostic

全球尚無經核准的 EBC 體外診斷項目，也沒有公認的判讀斷點。本平台定位為研究用途（RUO），這一點我們在第一頁就說清楚。

There is no approved EBC in-vitro diagnostic anywhere, and no accepted cut-off values. This platform is research use only — stated up front rather than buried.

這三篇合起來說的是同一件事Read together, these three say one thing

EBC 的成敗不在「怎麼採」，在「採來測什麼」。把灌洗液既有的蛋白分析物原封不動搬到 EBC——因為濃度低、稀釋不定——會失敗；改用對低濃度容忍度高的方法（核酸擴增、質譜／離子遷移譜這類圖譜式分析），訊號就出得來。這也是我們把研發重心放在圖譜式分析與模式識別、而不是單一蛋白定量的原因。

Whether EBC works is decided less by how you collect it than by what you measure in it. Transplanting an established protein assay from lavage fluid fails, because concentrations are low and dilution is variable; methods tolerant of low concentration — nucleic acid amplification, or pattern-based analysis such as mass spectrometry and ion mobility spectrometry — recover the signal. That is why our development focuses on spectral pattern analysis and pattern recognition rather than single-protein quantification.

EBC 目前公認的三個弱點（引自 ERS 2017 技術標準）Three acknowledged weaknesses of EBC (from the ERS 2017 technical standard)

  1. 稀釋無法校正。Dilution cannot be corrected. 冷凝液佔呼出氣體體積的比例每次都不同，且尚未證實用稀釋因子標準化能改善再現性。 The proportion of exhaled volume that condenses differs every time, and normalising by a dilution factor has not been shown to improve reproducibility.
    「1 mL 的 EBC 完全不能視為一個標準化的生物檢體，因為每次採集過程中冷凝液佔呼出氣體體積的百分比並不固定。」“1 mL EBC cannot be considered a standardised biological specimen at all, because the percentage of condensed liquid of the exhaled volume is not constant for each collection process.”
  2. 多數標記接近檢測下限。Most markers sit near the assay floor. 這直接放大了分析變異，也是上面那篇陰性研究失敗的主因之一。 This amplifies analytical variability, and is one reason the negative study above failed.
  3. 跨實驗室缺乏標準化。No cross-laboratory standardisation. 採樣方法與標記物量測都尚未在不同實驗室間完成驗證。ERS 該份文件並明言：「本報告無意提供疾病診斷與處置的臨床指引。」 Neither collection methods nor marker assays have been validated across laboratories. The ERS document states explicitly: “This report is not intended to provide clinical guidance on disease diagnosis and management.”

設計上的一個建議One suggestion on design

如果沒有特別理由，我們建議第一個研究從單次採樣、不需回診的設計開始。縱貫追蹤會同時放大收案、排程與流失率三種負擔，而 EBC 的圖譜品質要到第一批樣本進了質譜儀才看得出來。先確認訊號，再談追蹤設計，通常比較順。 Unless there is a specific reason not to, we suggest the first study uses a single collection visit with no follow-up. Longitudinal designs multiply recruitment, scheduling and attrition burden at once — and the quality of the EBC spectra is not visible until the first batch reaches the instrument. Confirming the signal first, then designing follow-up, usually goes more smoothly.

我們能提供的分析只有揮發性代謝體（VOC），以 GC-IMS 或 GC-MS 進行。EBC 檢體中的其他生化項目——細胞因子、pH、H₂O₂、8-isoprostane、核酸、蛋白定量等——須由貴院既有的實驗室設備或委外管道執行，我們不提供這些檢測，也不代為委外。若您的研究設計需要這些指標作為對照或共同終點，建議在計畫書撰寫階段就先確認院內量能與費用來源。

此外，本平台為研究用途（RUO）：我們回饋的是化學圖譜與分析結果，不產生任何個別受試者的疾病判讀，也不應寫入病歷或回報給受試者作為臨床資訊。

The only analysis we can provide is of volatile metabolites (VOCs), by GC-IMS or GC-MS. Every other assay on an EBC specimen — cytokines, pH, H₂O₂, 8-isoprostane, nucleic acids, protein quantification — must run on your institution’s own laboratory equipment or through your own outsourcing route. We do not perform these assays and cannot arrange them on your behalf. If your design needs any of them as a comparator or co-primary endpoint, confirm in-house capacity and funding while the protocol is being written.

This platform is also research use only: what we return are chemical spectra and analytical results. It produces no disease interpretation for any individual subject, and should not enter the medical record or be reported back to subjects as clinical information.
